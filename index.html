Our craft

Tea treated like produce, not product

Leaves are picked in narrow windows, rested, and cupped in-house before a single gram makes it onto the board. What doesn't sing doesn't ship.

Single-estate sourcing

Direct from 9 small farms across Uji, Nantou and Yunnan — traceable to the row.

Timed, never guessed

Every leaf has its own temperature and steep curve dialled in by our tea lead.

Low-waste kitchen

Spent leaves become our pastries' flavouring; cups are returnable for a discount.

From leaf to cup

Four steps, zero shortcuts

Shade & pick

Matcha fields are shaded for 20 days to build theanine and that deep jade colour.

Stone mill

Granite wheels turn at 30 rpm, yielding just 40g of powder per hour. Slow on purpose.

Whisk

A bamboo chasen lifts the powder into a fine foam — never a blender, never a shortcut.

Pour

Served at 78°C over milk, ice or nothing at all, depending on your mood.
