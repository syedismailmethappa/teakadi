Before you ask

Quick answers

Are dogs allowed?

On the terrace, yes — we keep a water bowl by the door and a puppuccino of oat foam.

Can I work here?

Absolutely, on weekdays before 4pm. We ask for one drink per hour and headphones after 11am.

Do you sell retail packs?

Yes — 50g tins of every pure tea, plus 30g matcha tins milled the day you buy them.

Is the space accessible?

Step-free entry, an accessible WC, and a lowered section of the bar counter.
